♡ How to Play

Jump over obstacles and collect stars!

Keyboard: Space / ↑ / W to jump. For Star Catch, use ← → or A / D. Bubble Pop: tap bubbles or press the screen.

On touch screens, tap the play area to move or interact. Use Play to pause and resume, and Retry for a fresh round.
